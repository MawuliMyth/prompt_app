Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prompt App collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, and profile details when you create an account.
  • Prompts, voice transcripts, saved history, favorites, and persona details you choose to add.
  • Messages you send for support or account help.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information such as operating system, app version, and basic diagnostics.
  • Usage and analytics events such as sign-in activity, prompt generation, paywall views, and feature usage.
  • Installation identifiers used to help prevent abuse of the free-trial flow.

Voice data

If you use voice input, audio is sent for transcription. Raw recordings are used to process your request and are not intended to be stored as a permanent audio archive by us. The resulting transcript may be stored as part of your prompt history.

2. How we use information

  • Provide prompt enhancement, transcription, saved history, and account features.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Enforce guest limits, free-user limits, and trial eligibility.
  • Improve app quality, reliability, and feature decisions.
  • Respond to support, legal, and security issues.

3. Third-party services

  • Firebase for authentication, Firestore storage, and analytics.
  • Anthropic Claude for prompt processing.
  • Groq Whisper for speech-to-text transcription.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In when you choose those sign-in methods.

Prompt App does not currently serve in-app advertising.

4. Billing and purchases

If paid subscriptions or purchases are enabled in a future release, billing will be processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, not directly by us. Store providers may share limited purchase status information needed to unlock premium access.

5. Data storage and retention

  • Account and prompt data may remain available until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Analytics and operational logs may be retained for security, troubleshooting, and product improvement.
  • You can request account deletion from within the app when eligible.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain uses of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

7. Children's privacy

Prompt App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be posted at this page.
