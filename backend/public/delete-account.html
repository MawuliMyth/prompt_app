Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Prompt App lets users delete their account and associated app data from inside the app. You can also contact us if you need help with account deletion.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open Prompt App.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Open the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete account and confirm the deletion.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, Prompt App removes your account access and deletes saved app data associated with your account, including saved prompts, prompt history, favourites, and persona details where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Some limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, analytics aggregation, or operational logs. These records are not used to recreate your account.
